## Start here

**[Murmur](https://github.com/rgentul/murmur)** is a local Mac reader for voice memo transcripts. It has a memo reader, search across every memo, a map of recorded days, and review of the tasks and events you mentioned. It runs on a made-up sample library, with one command and nothing to install. Nothing leaves your Mac.

The version I use every day also turns my voice memos into those transcripts. This public version doesn't.
